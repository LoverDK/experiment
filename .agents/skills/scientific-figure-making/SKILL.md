# Scientific figure making

Open `references/` only as needed; do not preload every file.

## When to load this skill

- Matplotlib figures for papers, slides, or reports that must match the figures4papers publication look.
- Grouped bars, trend lines, heatmaps, multi-panel grids, or PDF/SVG/high-DPI scientific output.
- References to figures4papers `figure_*` projects.

## When not to load

- Plotly, Altair, Bokeh, or other interactive/web-first plotting.
- EDA-only plots without a publication target.
- Primary 3D/GIS/non-matplotlib tooling.
- Illustrator/Figma-first layout.
